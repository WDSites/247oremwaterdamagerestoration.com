Crawl space water damage cleanup in Orem

Crawl space water damage cleanup is the pumping, removal of wet materials and drying of the low space under a floor, for homes in Orem, UT. The dispatch line is open 24/7, and the cleanup matters now because floor joists, subfloor and insulation start taking on moisture as soon as water sits below them. While you wait, stay out of the crawl space, shut off any known leak source and note whether the floor above feels soft or cold.

This page is about water under the house. Standing water in a finished basement or living area belongs on the emergency water removal page, and mold is a follow-on topic, not the first problem.

When water under the house is an emergency

Water that you can see through the access hatch, a sump pit that is overflowing or a floor that has gone spongy overhead is an emergency. Night does not slow it. The line stays staffed on weekends and after hours, and we aim for same-day response when available.

The slow cases matter as much. A damp odor in the hallway, cold floors in one corner or insulation hanging down in sheets means moisture has been building for a while. Call for a moisture reading before the framing stays wet for weeks.

Where the water comes from

Crawl spaces take on water four ways. A supply or drain line under the floor leaks, runoff or snowmelt seeps in through the foundation, a sump pump overflows or quits, or a torn vapor barrier lets ground moisture rise. Plumbing leaks are the quickest and clean at first, and seepage is slower and can bring dirt.

Orem sits near the eastern shore of Utah Lake, so ask the city about drainage conditions for your block before assuming anything about your lot. Crawl spaces are one foundation type here, and basements are common in Orem too. If you have a basement and it floods during runoff, the cleanup falls under flood damage restoration.

Orem Public Works advised residents in the high-runoff spring of 2023 to clear gutters, curbs and storm drains and to aim downspouts and sump pump discharge away from the house. That advice protects a crawl space as much as a basement, so extend downspouts, keep discharge hoses pointed well away from the foundation and make sure window wells drain.

Crawl space safety steps

  • Never enter standing water in a crawl space that has wiring, outlets or a sump pump nearby.
  • Turn off power to the crawl space circuit only if the panel is dry and reachable without crossing water.
  • Call 911 or the utility for sparking, gas smell or sewage rising near the hatch.
  • Low clearance, damp insulation and loose dirt make the space hard to leave quickly, so do not go in alone.
  • Close the main water shutoff if you suspect a supply line is leaking, then call dispatch.

How the crew pumps and dries a crawl space

  1. Assess the entry

    The crew checks access, clearance and any wiring or ductwork near the floor before anyone goes under.

  2. Pump and extract

    Submersible pumps and portable extraction units pull standing water through the hatch or a vent opening.

  3. Remove wet insulation

    Soaked batts and loose debris come out, since wet insulation holds water against the joists for days.

  4. Read the framing

    Moisture meters measure joists and subfloor, and a thermal imaging camera from above shows wet areas in the floor.

  5. Dry the space

    LGR dehumidifiers and air movers are set for the low volume, with ducting or ventilation as needed. The method is explained on the structural drying page, and the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard is the reference for drying goals.

  6. Treat and recheck

    An antimicrobial treatment goes on affected wood where needed. Readings repeat until the framing is dry.

Vapor barrier and insulation decisions

Once the space is dry, you decide what goes back. A vapor barrier covers bare ground, and insulation sits between joists. Each choice has trade-offs.

Rebuilding and repairs after drying fall under water damage repair, and permits for plumbing or electrical work may apply through Orem's Building Safety Division.

Prevention and mistakes to avoid

Do not run a fan into a wet crawl space and call it dry, because a warm, humid day will pull more moisture in. Do not pile gutter water against the foundation.

Check your hatch each season. In Pleasant Grove and Lindon, grading, window wells and downspout runs deserve a look every spring.
