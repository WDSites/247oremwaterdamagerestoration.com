Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Orem, UT

A leak under the sink or behind the dishwasher soaks cabinets and subfloor fast, and Orem kitchens can be dried and restored.

What kitchen water damage restoration covers

Kitchen water damage restoration is the drying, cleaning and repair planning for the cabinets, floors, walls and counters of a kitchen after water gets in, and it is one of the most common room-level calls in Orem, UT. Our dispatch line runs around the clock, so a Saturday night leak is not left to sit.

This page is about the room and what the water does to its materials. The source can be anything, and if the culprit was a washer or fridge line, appliance leak cleanup covers the hoses and valves. Once drying is complete, rebuilding falls under water damage repair.

Why kitchens take the hardest hit

A kitchen packs the sink, dishwasher, disposal, refrigerator line and often a range hood into one tight footprint, and most of those connections sit inside closed cabinets. A weeping fitting can run for weeks before anyone sees it. Water then collects on the cabinet floor, travels under the toe-kick and soaks into the subfloor.

Vineyard is a good local example. Its housing grew from 139 residents in 2010 to 12,543 in 2020, so most kitchens there are recent construction with particleboard cabinet boxes and thin underlayment. In Vineyard and similar newer neighborhoods, check under the sink and behind the toe-kick every few months, and ask the builder or warranty holder about the fittings.

When to call about a wet kitchen

Call as soon as you find swelling, a soft floor or standing water, even if the leak itself looks minor. Particleboard drinks water quickly, and each day of dampness raises the chance that boxes, not just the floor, need replacing. Quick drying is what keeps a repair small.

Shut the water at the angle stop under the sink first. If that does not stop it, close the home's main shutoff. A main-line break before the meter is the city's job, so call the Orem water line in that case.

Kitchen water safety before you start

Kitchens mix water with outlets, range cords and appliances. Do not step in water near a plugged-in dishwasher, fridge or microwave. If the circuit panel is dry and safe to reach, switch off the kitchen breakers, and if you see sparks, smell gas or hear buzzing, leave and call 911 or the utility before calling us.

Keep in mind that wet cabinets and floors are slick, and a loaded upper cabinet can shift when the wall behind it is soaked. Empty them from the top down.

How the crew dries and restores a kitchen

  1. Find where the water went

    Moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera trace water from the leak through cabinets, wall bottoms and the floor assembly.

  2. Extract surface water

    Portable extraction units pull standing water off tile, vinyl and the cabinet floor before drying begins.

  3. Open the hidden spaces

    Toe-kicks come off, and small access holes may be cut or drilled behind or under cabinets so air can reach the wet cavity.

  4. Sort save from replace

    Solid wood doors and tile can often be kept, while swollen particleboard boxes and soaked underlayment usually cannot be dried back to shape.

  5. Dry with air movers and dehumidifiers

    Air movers push air into the opened cavities, and LGR dehumidifiers pull moisture out, per the drying practices in ANSI/IICRC S500.

  6. Verify and hand off for repair

    Readings are taken each visit until the materials match a dry reference, then the work moves to rebuilding.

Drying behind cabinets and the permit question

Dry cabinets often depend on whether air reaches the hidden cavity. For the method behind the equipment and why it takes days, see structural drying and dehumidification.

Rebuilding a kitchen can involve plumbing, electrical and cabinet work. According to Orem's Building Safety Division, permits apply to work that remodels or repairs, plus plumbing and electrical work, with some exceptions. Ask Building Safety before the first cabinet comes out, and ask your contractor who pulls the permit.

What shapes the cost of a kitchen restoration

Cabinet material and run length

Plywood boxes can often be dried, while particleboard runs may be replaced as a set.

Flooring type

Tile, vinyl plank, laminate and hardwood each dry differently, and some must be removed and reinstalled.

How far the water traveled

A leak that reached the dining room floor means more drying equipment and longer monitoring.

How long it sat

A slow leak found late affects more layers than a sudden event handled the same day.

Access around fixtures

Built-in appliances, islands and counters add labor when they have to be moved or protected.

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a homeowners policy, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and photograph the cabinets and floor before anything is removed.

Mistakes to avoid in a wet kitchen

Do not caulk or paint over a swollen toe-kick, since that traps water. Do not leave a dishwasher running to check whether it still leaks. Do not slide a heavy appliance across a soft floor, which can tear flooring and crack a supply line.
