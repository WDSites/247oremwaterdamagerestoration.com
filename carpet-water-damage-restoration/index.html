Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Orem, UT

Wet carpet in an Orem home can often be saved if the water is clean and the drying starts quickly, but the pad rarely survives.

What carpet water damage restoration means

Carpet water damage restoration is the process of extracting water from carpet, pad and the floor beneath, then drying, cleaning and treating what stays and removing what cannot be saved. It is a routine call in Orem, UT, because carpet covers most bedrooms and many finished basements. Our dispatch line runs 24/7 for wet floors.

This page is about the flooring layer. The wider cleanup of debris and contaminated materials sits on the water damage cleanup page, and the drying science is explained under structural drying and dehumidification.

How carpet gets wet in Orem homes

Typical sources are a washer hose, an overflowing tub, a water heater that split, a window well that filled in spring, or a ceiling that dripped for a night. Carpet over a concrete slab behaves differently from carpet over a wood subfloor, and finished basements carry both the carpet and the cold concrete below it.

If the water came from a failed main line before your meter, the city handles that side. Orem's water system serves about 23,530 connections through the Water Section of Public Works, and a main-line break ahead of the meter is a call to the city line. Past the meter, the break belongs to you, so locate your shutoff before you need it.

When to call about wet carpet

Call the same day if you can feel water underfoot. Carpet that stays wet past a couple of days starts to smell, the tack strip rusts, and mold can begin to grow where moisture lingers. A fast response keeps the job to extraction and drying instead of replacement.

Lift furniture legs onto foil or blocks, and take up small rugs, but do not pull the carpet yourself unless you know it is clean water.

Wet carpet safety steps

Wet carpet conducts electricity from any cord or outlet it touches. Unplug what you can reach from dry ground, and cut power at the panel only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. If you see sparks or smell gas, leave and call 911 or the utility first.

If the water is dark or smells like sewage, stay out of the room, keep shoes and pets away from it, and read the sewage backup cleanup page. Ask a physician about any exposure.

How the crew handles wet carpet

  1. Classify the water

    Clean, gray or contaminated water decides whether carpet can be dried in place or has to go.

  2. Extract from the surface

    Truck-mounted or portable extraction units with a wand pull water out of the face fibers and the pad.

  3. Lift and inspect

    The carpet is peeled back from the tack strip so the pad and the subfloor can be checked with a moisture meter and a thermal imaging camera.

  4. Remove the pad

    A saturated pad is usually removed because it holds water and does not dry well under carpet.

  5. Dry the carpet and floor

    Floating the carpet on air movers lets air reach the backing, while LGR dehumidifiers pull moisture from the room, following ANSI/IICRC S500.

  6. Treat and reset

    Antimicrobial treatment goes on the carpet and subfloor, then the carpet is re-stretched on new pad once the readings are dry.

Dry in place, float or replace

The choice rests on the water category, how long the carpet sat wet and what is under it.

OptionWhen it fitsTrade-off
Dry in placeClean water, caught early, pad can be treated or is thinLeast disruption, but hidden moisture may remain
Float the carpetClean water, carpet lifted and pad removedSaves the carpet but needs stretching afterward
Replace carpet and padCategory 2 or 3 water, or carpet that delaminatedHighest certainty, more rebuilding work

Be honest about the pad. In most wet-carpet jobs it is removed, because foam holds water and dries slowly. Even when the carpet is saved, plan on a new pad and a stretch.

Concrete, wood subfloors and rentals

Over a slab, water can sit between pad and concrete for days, so drying has to reach the slab. Over wood, the subfloor may swell, cup or stain. Moisture readings on both, repeated each visit, show when the floor is ready for new pad.

Near Utah Valley University, many Orem rentals have carpet throughout. Tenants should report water to the landlord in writing and photograph the carpet before it is touched, and landlords should keep the same records for their insurer. For similar rental questions in American Fork or Pleasant Grove, the same documentation advice holds.

What shapes the cost of carpet restoration

Water category

Clean water can leave carpet in place, while contaminated water means removal and more cleaning.

Square footage wet

A single bedroom needs fewer air movers than a basement family room.

Pad and carpet quality

Thin builder-grade carpet and dense pad dry and reinstall differently.

Time since the leak

Carpet wet for a day is easier to save than carpet wet for a week.

Subfloor type

Slabs, plywood and older plank subfloors take different drying time.

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a homeowners policy, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and photograph the wet area first.

Mistakes to avoid and the permit note

Do not run a steam cleaner over water-soaked carpet, which adds moisture. Do not lay a plastic sheet over it, which traps the damp. Do not wait for the pad to smell before you call.

Replacing carpet alone usually needs no permit, but plumbing or electrical repair done during the rebuild can. Orem's Building Safety Division is the place to ask.
