Appliance leak cleanup in Orem homes

Appliance leak water damage cleanup is the extraction, drying and sanitizing of water that escaped from a washing machine, dishwasher, refrigerator line, water softener or humidifier in Orem, UT. The water is usually clean at first, which gives you a short window. Our dispatch line runs 24/7, and until the crew gets to you, shut off the valve behind the unit, unplug it only if the floor around the outlet is dry, and lift rugs up.

Cleaning up after a broken kitchen sink or cabinet is a different job, with its own page on kitchen water damage restoration. This page is about the machine and the room it sits in.

Which appliance leaked and why it matters

Washing machine

Braided supply hoses split at the crimped ends, and drain hoses pop off the standpipe during a spin cycle. Both pump water at full house pressure.

Dishwasher

The door gasket, the inlet valve and the drain connection under the sink are the usual weak points. Water often travels under the flooring before anyone notices.

Refrigerator and ice maker

A thin plastic or copper line behind the unit can crack or pop off. It leaks slowly and may soak a subfloor for weeks.

Softener and humidifier

Brine lines, drain tubes and reservoirs overflow in utility rooms, closets and garages where nobody checks them often.

When an appliance leak needs a quick call

A washer hose that lets go is the fast version. Hot water at full pressure can fill a laundry room in minutes and run into a hallway before you reach the valve. Water that reaches carpet, a ceiling below or a wall cavity needs a call right away, day or night.

Slow leaks are the quiet version. A dishwasher gasket or fridge line may drip for weeks behind a unit, so the first sign is a soft floor, a musty smell or a stain on the ceiling underneath. Ask for same-day response when available, and tell the dispatcher how long you think it has been leaking, since that shapes what the crew checks first.

First-hour safety steps

  • Close the valves behind the appliance. If they are stuck, use the home's main shutoff.
  • Do not unplug a unit while standing in water. Turn off its circuit at the breaker only if the panel is dry.
  • Keep children and pets out of the room, since wet laundry floors are slick.
  • Call 911 or the utility for sparking, a gas smell or water coming through light fixtures.
  • Lift rugs, baskets and electronics off the floor, then call dispatch.

How the crew dries the room and what it uses

  1. Shut off and verify

    The crew confirms the line is closed and the appliance is not still feeding water.

  2. Extract

    Portable extraction units pull water from vinyl, tile and carpet. Deep basement water may call for the pumping described on the water damage cleanup page.

  3. Read the room

    Moisture meters check baseboards, drywall and subfloor, and a thermal imaging camera shows wet areas hiding under the machine and behind trim.

  4. Open and remove

    Wet baseboard, swollen particleboard and soaked pad are cut out when they cannot dry in place.

  5. Dry and treat

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run for days, not hours, following the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard as a reference for drying goals. An antimicrobial treatment goes on where needed.

  6. Re-read and release

    Moisture readings are repeated until the materials match a dry reference.

What drives the cost of an appliance leak cleanup

Slow or sudden

A slow leak usually soaked more material before anyone saw it, which adds removal and drying.

Floors below

Water that reached a finished ceiling downstairs adds a second area to dry.

Flooring type

Vinyl and tile dry quickly, while laminate, carpet pad and hardwood often do not.

Equipment and days

Air mover and dehumidifier counts follow the readings and the floor plan.

Rebuild after drying

Replacing flooring and trim is a separate repair scope. If the water came from a tank instead, see the water heater leak page.

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. A split hose and a seeping gasket may be treated very differently, so check your policy or ask your insurer, and never assume approval.

Prevention and mistakes to avoid

Replace rubber washer hoses with braided stainless models every few years and close the valves when you travel. Set a drain pan under the water heater and fridge if the floor below is finished.
