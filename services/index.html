Six services, six different jobs

People call us with a wet floor, a stained ceiling or a musty closet, and the right service depends on what stage the problem is in. A pipe that is still running needs a different crew response than a wall that dried last month and now needs new drywall. This page lays out the six services we dispatch to Orem, UT and explains how to choose between them.

Every service here is carried out by licensed and insured restoration professionals sent through a single line that answers around the clock. Water damage is our main work. Mold remediation is secondary, and it usually follows a water problem that was never fully dried.

What each service is for

Water damage repair

The rebuild. Once the structure reads dry, water damage repair replaces ceilings, walls, insulation, flooring and cabinets. Choose it when the water is gone and you can see what is left to fix.

Water mitigation

The first three days. Water mitigation limits the spread by finding the source, starting drying equipment and measuring moisture in walls you cannot see through.

Water damage cleanup

The clearing out. Water damage cleanup removes soaked materials, sanitizes what remains and deals with contents. Choose it after the leak has been stopped.

Emergency water removal

The pumps. Emergency water removal is for standing water you can see, such as inches on a basement floor or a flooded laundry room.

Flood damage restoration

The outside water. Flood damage restoration handles runoff, snowmelt, river overflow, groundwater and sewage, all of which carry contamination.

Mold remediation

The containment job. Mold remediation removes mold growth under containment after the moisture source has been fixed.

How the services connect on a real job

Most losses use more than one service, in a set order.

  1. Pumps and extraction first

    Standing water comes out with submersible pumps and extraction units, which is the emergency water removal stage.

  2. Mitigation sets up the drying

    Air movers, LGR dehumidifiers and moisture meters turn the job into a measured drying project.

  3. Cleanup removes what cannot be saved

    Wet carpet pad, soaked insulation and swollen drywall are cut out and bagged.

  4. Repair rebuilds

    Once readings are normal, new drywall, paint and flooring go in. Rebuilds may need permits, so ask the Orem Building Safety Division.

  5. Mold remediation only if growth appears

    This applies when materials stayed wet. It works under containment with negative air and HEPA air scrubbers.

Choosing between two close services

Mitigation and cleanup overlap. Mitigation is about stopping progress and drying the structure, while cleanup is about removing and sanitizing. If the water is clean and recent, mitigation often covers most of it. If it came from a washer, a sump overflow or a sewer line, cleanup carries more of the work.

Emergency water removal and flood damage restoration also overlap. A burst pipe in a basement is typically removal. A basement that filled because snowmelt backed up through a window well is flood damage, because the water is outside water with contaminants in it.

Repair is often confused with mitigation, but they sit at opposite ends of the job. Do not close up walls until the drying is verified, since sealing wet drywall behind fresh paint hides a problem that returns as stains or mold.

What we do not do

Restoration is not waterproofing. If water keeps entering through a foundation, ask a waterproofing or foundation contractor. We also do not do roofing, and we do not diagnose health conditions or offer equipment rentals.
