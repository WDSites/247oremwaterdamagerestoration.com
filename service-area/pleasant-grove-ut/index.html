If water is still spreading in Pleasant Grove

You can stop most leaks at the valve

Turn the fixture valve clockwise, or close the main where the line comes into the house. A creek or runoff problem has no valve, so move to sandbags, a clear gutter and a pump decision instead.

2

You should switch off power only if you can reach a dry panel

Step on dry floor only. If the panel stands in water or water is touching outlets, back out of the building and call 911 or the utility. Wait for them before phoning us.

3

You can raise the small things

Move paperwork, medicine, chargers and keepsakes up to a counter or second floor. Boxes in a basement go onto shelves or stairs.

4

You should keep family out of the flood

Runoff, creek water and anything from a drain can be contaminated. Children and pets wait upstairs, and nobody walks through standing water in bare feet.

5

You should call the all-hours line

Say where the water is entering, how deep it is and whether the sump pump is running. Take dated photos while the crew is on the way.

What the Grove Creek runoff taught the city

On a Sunday in May 2005, Grove Creek runoff exceeded expectations after a channel carrying water from a debris basin to a canal backed up and eroded the canal banks. Officials routed the water down Fifth North (500 North), tearing up pavement. About 450 volunteers and city employees filled sandbags from a supply of about 25,000, and no homes had been damaged as of that afternoon.

The lesson for homeowners is that bench creeks can change course in hours. Check lot grading so water slopes away from the foundation, keep window wells covered and clear, and point downspouts a few feet beyond the wall. Sandbags only help if you stage them before the water arrives.
