A built-out Orem neighborhood

Windsor is a neighborhood in Orem, UT made up mostly of owner-occupied single-family homes. This page explains what Orem's neighborhood plan says about housing and drainage here, then gives first steps for water in the house.

Windsor shares its plan with Northridge and Heatheridge, about 2,200 housing units across the three. For the city view, see Orem water damage restoration and the Orem service area page.

Where the plan puts Windsor

The plan places its three neighborhoods between State Street, Main Street, 1600 North, 2000 North and 1200 North. Windsor is census block group E and most of block group F. Landmarks include Windsor Park and Windsor Elementary.

Two block groups, two build eras

Block group E

Median year built is 1981, and 73.8% of homes are owner-occupied with 26.2% rented. If you rent here, tell your landlord about any leak in writing.

Block group F

Median year built is 1995, and 90.0% of homes are owner-occupied. Most repairs and claim decisions here sit with the homeowner.

Amiron Village

Sixty-eight units in 17 quadplexes make this the largest multifamily development in the plan. A leak in one unit can reach a neighbor, so tell the property manager.

Plan-wide pattern

Single-family detached homes predominate, and every block group is at least 70% owner-occupied. Build eras use different materials, so a restoration crew reads each house on site.

Know your drainage and your yard

The Public Works map shows a Windsor detention basin and the Timp detention basin, and Orem has about 20 basins citywide, according to Fox13. The plan does not address basements or flooding, so check your own house.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your exact address, because a zone applies to mapped areas and not to a whole neighborhood. Zone X does not guarantee a dry house.

If rain or runoff gets inside, storm water damage cleanup applies. Ask Orem Public Works about public drainage.

How drying works in a house from either era

  1. Stop the source

    Shut the water off if safe. Burst pipe water damage cleanup begins with the valve closed.

  2. Remove standing water

    Extraction pulls water from carpet and floors first. See water damage cleanup.

  3. Dry with measured airflow

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers work together, as covered under structural drying and dehumidification.

  4. Take readings until dry

    A restoration crew repeats moisture readings and adjusts equipment. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference standard for this work.

Safety rules for water in a Windsor house

Keep people and pets out of contaminated or electrically hazardous water. Shut off the water only if the valve is safe and reachable. Never touch breakers or use electrical equipment while standing in water.

Call 911 or the utility for sparks, a gas smell or sewage at outlets. Photograph when safe, and move unaffected items only when safe.

Sewage and storm water are Category 3. Do not dry them yourself, and avoid shop vacs and household fans.

Insurance, claims and records

Coverage depends on the cause and your policy terms, so ask your insurer. The Herald Extra reported in 2023 that most homeowners insurance does not cover flooding and flood insurance takes 30 days to take effect.

Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope help document the loss.
