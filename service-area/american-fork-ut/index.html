When water is spreading in American Fork

River water, snowmelt or a failed pipe is spreading through an American Fork house, and the next hour decides how much drywall you save. Call dispatch from a safe spot. Crews work from our Orem office, about 9 miles south, and the restoration professionals we send are licensed and insured.

This town is named for a waterway. The American Fork River runs through the city, so a good first question for any wet basement is whether the water came from inside the walls or from outside the lot.

The river, the canyon and your lot

The river drains American Fork Canyon at the foot of Mount Timpanogos and reaches town north of Utah Lake. In spring, snowmelt from above fills the channel quickly. Homes near the river should check the sump pump, clear gutters and storm grates and know where the nearest low spot is before runoff peaks.

Robinson Park is one of the open spaces residents use along the way, and the river is easy to watch from town. If your lot is near water, photograph the yard now, so you have a before record for any later claim.

What the 2023 runoff season took to manage

In 2023 the city's Sewer and Storm Drain department and American Fork Irrigation Company employees led a flood prevention team. Starting in February they cleared the river channel, repaired eroding walls, staged supplies at problem spots, cleaned storm drain grates and kept the debris basin and weir clear. They monitored the river around the clock and filled over 5,000 sandbags with volunteers.

That list is a ready-made checklist for homeowners. Clear the grate in front of your lot, keep leaves out of gutters, and do the work in February, not in May. Your own sump discharge should run well past the foundation.

If water is still spreading in American Fork

1

Source first

Close the fixture valve, or the main if the leak is bigger. When the water is coming from the river or the yard instead, there is no valve to close, so protect doors with sandbags and clear the nearest grate.

2

Panel check, then breakers

Dry floor, easy reach, and you may switch off the circuits to the wet rooms. Wet panel, wet outlets or sewage? Out of the building, then 911 or the utility, then us.

3

Valuables upward

Anything you would hate to lose goes higher: files, electronics, photographs, medicine, kids' shoes. Basement boxes go up the stairs.

4

Hands, feet and paws out

River and storm water may be contaminated, and sewer water certainly is. The household and the pets stay in dry rooms.

5

Then the line

Our 24/7 line takes the details: where the water enters, how high, and whether the sump pump has power. Take a few dated photos before the crew arrives.

Storm grates, debris basins and sump discharge

A clogged storm grate pushes street water toward driveways and garage doors. The city's work with its debris basin and weir shows how much depends on small outlets staying open. If you see a grate buried in leaves, clear it or report it.

Sump pumps fail during the worst storms, so test yours each spring with a bucket of water and check the discharge line. A pipe that dumps next to the foundation sends the same water back into the basement.

Canyon, temple and summer streets

American Fork was settled in 1850 and renamed in 1860, and the Mount Timpanogos Utah Temple was dedicated in 1996. Steel Days each summer draws crowds, and summer also brings air conditioner condensate lines that back up and drip through ceilings. Clear the drain pan before guests arrive.

The Columbia Steel plant from World War II closed in November 2001, and the town has kept growing since. Housing ages vary, so tell the dispatcher how old your house is before the crew arrives.
