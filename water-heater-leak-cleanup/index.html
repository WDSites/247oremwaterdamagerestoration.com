Water heater leak cleanup in Orem

Water heater leak cleanup is the removal of leaked tank water from floors, walls and stored belongings, followed by drying and sanitizing, for homes in Orem, UT. A failed tank can release a large volume across a utility room quickly, so treat this as an emergency. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, and while you wait you can close the cold supply valve, lift boxes off the floor and keep everyone out of wet rooms near the panel.

This page covers the water that came out of the heater. We clean up and dry that water. We do not repair or replace water heaters, so a plumber still has to deal with the tank itself.

When a leaking water heater is an emergency

A damp ring around the base is a warning. A steady stream, a hissing sound, or a pool spreading toward carpet is an emergency. Water heaters tend to fail late at night, and the night does not change the damage, so the line stays staffed after hours and on weekends.

We aim for same-day response when available, and the dispatcher will explain honestly what that looks like for your address. Water that reaches drywall begins wicking upward right away. Insulation around a closet wall holds water like a sponge, and finished flooring starts to lift once it has soaked for a day or two.

Basement mechanical rooms are the worst case, since the water runs to the lowest point and soaks the framing and any carpet nearby. If the tank sits upstairs or in an attic closet, check the ceiling below for stains and sag.

How water heaters fail in Orem homes

A tank fails in one of three ways. The steel shell corrodes through at a seam, a fitting or the drain valve loosens, or the temperature and pressure relief valve discharges because of excess pressure. Hard-water scale inside the tank speeds up the first kind, and Utah Valley tends to run on the hard side.

Orem's culinary water is managed by the Water Section of Public Works, which serves roughly 23,530 connections according to the city's 2022 water conservation plan. Know where your home's main shutoff sits and where the city meter is. If water is coming from the street side of the meter, call the city, and if it is on your side, it is yours to stop.

Vineyard, about 3 miles west of Orem near Utah Lake, grew from 139 residents in 2010 to 12,543 in 2020, so much of its housing is recent construction. In newer homes, the usual failures are water heaters, supply lines and fixtures. If you live in Vineyard and the heater is young, ask the builder or manufacturer's warranty holder about the failed part before anything is thrown out, and photograph the tank and label first.

First-hour safety steps

  • Smell gas or hear hissing near a gas unit? Leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before anything else.
  • Never step into standing water if the panel, outlets or the heater's electrical connection are wet.
  • Turn off power to an electric heater at the breaker only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without crossing water.
  • Close the cold supply valve on top of the tank, or the home's main shutoff if the valve is stuck.
  • Leave the hot water faucet closed. Draining the tank from the spigot spreads more water.
  • Lift cardboard boxes, shoes and electronics off the floor, then call the dispatch line.

How the crew removes the water and dries the room

  1. Confirm the source is stopped

    The crew checks that the supply is shut and the unit is isolated so no new water arrives during extraction.

  2. Extract standing water

    Portable extraction units and wet vacuums pull water from the floor. Larger spills in a basement may need a submersible pump, which is covered on the emergency water removal page.

  3. Map the damage

    Moisture meters read wet drywall, baseboard and subfloor, and a thermal imaging camera shows where water ran behind finished walls.

  4. Remove what cannot dry

    Saturated insulation, soft baseboard and swollen particleboard shelving are cut out. Clean tank water is Category 1, but it becomes worse if left in porous materials.

  5. Set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers

    Air movers push dry air across surfaces while LGR dehumidifiers pull moisture from the air. Crews follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard as a reference for drying goals.

  6. Clean, treat and recheck

    Surfaces get cleaned and an antimicrobial treatment goes on where needed. Readings are repeated until the materials match a dry reference.

What shapes the cost of a water heater leak cleanup

How far the water travelled

A spill held inside a tiled utility room is a smaller job than one that crossed into carpet or a finished basement.

Flooring and wall materials

Concrete dries quickly while carpet pad, hardwood and drywall take longer or need removal.

Time between failure and the call

Water that sat overnight has soaked deeper than water caught in the first hour.

Drying equipment and days on site

The number of air movers and dehumidifiers and how long they run depends on the readings, not a fixed schedule.

Rebuild needs afterward

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a homeowners policy, while slow leaks and neglected equipment often are not. Mistakes to avoid
